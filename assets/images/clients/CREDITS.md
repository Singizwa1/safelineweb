## Licence

These are used under the [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/), which
permits **free commercial use**, allows modification (these were cropped and
resized), and does **not require attribution**. Nothing was purchased and there is
no per-use fee or renewal.

Two restrictions in that licence are worth knowing, because they apply to how you
may reuse these files elsewhere:

- You may not sell unmodified copies of the photos.
- You may not imply that the people depicted endorse a product, **nor use them in
  a way that is defamatory or offensive to the people shown**.

The current use — as generic customer portraits beside written testimonials — is
standard practice for stock imagery. If you ever want a named, quotable
endorsement from a real customer, use a photo of that actual customer with their
written permission instead.

## Replacing them

Each file is a 320×320 JPEG, cropped to the face. The CSS renders them in a 56px
circle with `object-fit: cover`, so a replacement of any aspect ratio will still
sit correctly — just keep the filename.

Real SafeLine customer photos (with their permission) would be better than stock
and can be dropped straight in over these.
